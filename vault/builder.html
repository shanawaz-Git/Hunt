create new vault

Start from scratch. You will set a passcode for each person who gets access.

~/settings

What shows on the home screen after unlocking.

~/access

Each person gets their own passcode. The vault greets them by name. Passcodes are never stored — if every passcode is forgotten, the vault cannot be opened. Keep a plaintext backup (export tab) somewhere safe offline.

Re-key = new encryption key, everyone sets a passcode again. Use it to fully revoke someone who already had access.

~/memories

Shown as a timeline, oldest first. Photo is optional.

~/gallery

Photos are resized to 1600px and compressed before encryption. Use JPG/PNG/WebP (convert iPhone HEIC first).

~/notes

Letters, inside jokes, promises. Line breaks are kept.

~/dates

Yearly dates (birthdays, anniversaries) count down to the next one. One-time dates count down, or show days since.
